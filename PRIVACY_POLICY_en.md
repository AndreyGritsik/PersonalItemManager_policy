# Privacy Policy for Personal Item Manager

**Last Updated:** March 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Personal Item Manager** ("we", "us", or "our"), application package `com.agrand.personalitemmanager`, collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

---

## 1. Summary

Personal Item Manager is designed with user privacy as a priority. All your personal items, categories, storage places, collections, notes, and photos remain stored **locally on your device**. We do not operate external databases to store your personal inventory or track your personal item data.

---

## 2. Information Collected and Used

### A. Local User Data (On-Device Only)
The App stores the following data locally on your device:
* **Item Details:** Names, descriptions, prices, weights, manufacture dates, custom categories, notes, and storage place hierarchies.
* **Photos & Images:** Photos taken or selected for your items and collections. Images are stored in local app storage or cache.
* **Backup Files:** Optional ZIP backup files created by you and saved to your device's storage.

*None of your local item details, notes, or photos are uploaded to our servers.*

### B. Camera & Hardware Access
* **Camera Permission (`android.permission.CAMERA`):** Required only if you choose to take photos of your personal items directly within the App. Photos remain strictly on your device.

### C. Third-Party Advertising & Analytics (Google AdMob)
The App uses **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)** to display banner and interstitial advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process information about your device, including:
* Device identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID).
* IP address.
* Coarse location data (country/city level).
* App performance and diagnostic information.

For more information about how Google processes advertising data, please review:
* [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* [How Google uses information from apps or sites that use their services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

### D. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)
The App uses the **Google Play Billing Library** to process optional in-app purchases (e.g., extending item limits and removing ads).
* All financial transactions and payment data are handled securely by Google Play.
* We do not collect or store credit card or financial information.

---

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion

* **Local Data:** All your inventory data remains on your device until you delete individual items, clear app data, or uninstall the App.
* **Backup Files:** Backup files created by the export tool remain in your chosen location until deleted by you.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy

Personal Item Manager does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

---

## 5. Security

We take reasonable measures to safeguard your data. Because all item inventory data is stored locally on your device, the security of your local data also depends on your device security settings (such as passcode, PIN, or biometric locks).

---

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date above.
